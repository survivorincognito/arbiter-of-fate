Getting Started · a quick guide to your first expedition

The Director records your decisions; it does not read or alter your Skyrim save.

  1. Create your character. Choose a race and playstyle, and save them.
  2. Choose your rules. Set Perk Focus and how much control Fate has. Magic and shouts are optional tracking systems.
  3. Check installed content. Enable only the game content you have.
  4. Discover quests. Find a quest in All and select Discover. It moves to Discovered.
  5. Ask Fate to choose. Draw from your eligible quests; complete or block the assigned objective when appropriate. Unverified quests require manual confirmation first.
  6. Keep a backup. Your progress saves in this browser. Export a JSON backup and keep it somewhere safe before clearing browser data, changing devices, or replacing the file.

Quest starting conditions are not all verified. Check that each objective can actually be started in your Skyrim game.

How shout tracking works

Each shout contains three words. Mark Discovered after you learn a word in-game, then Unlocked when you can actually use it. Most word unlocks consume dragon souls, but story-granted words may be unlocked for free.

Shouts are separate from the five magic schools. Perk Focus and spell restrictions do not prohibit them. The tracker does not impose extra Fate restrictions.

The standard catalogue includes 20 base-game shouts, three Dawnguard shouts and four Dragonborn shouts. Mods can introduce additional ones.

How to Use The Fate Director

THE ARBITER OF FATE

A practical guide to your first expedition and the systems you'll return to later. Open only the chapters you need; none of these instructions changes your save or asks Fate to make a decision.

1. Set up your expedition · edition, race and starting build
  1. In Settings → Edition and Content, select your Skyrim edition and the content actually installed, then save. The Director cannot detect your installation.
  2. In Character, choose a race, a starting playstyle, and a name; save your character details. A new, unprogressed level-one character receives the race's standard starting skill levels.
  3. Choose a Perk Focus under Character to determine which perk trees Fate may draw from. You can fine-tune the trees; an edited preset becomes Custom.

Example: A Wood Elf choosing Stealth Archer starts with the Wood Elf skill bonuses. Adding Destruction to the focus changes it to Custom; restoring the exact original trees restores the Stealth Archer preset. Equipment suggestions are optional, not forced restrictions.

2. Decide what The Arbiter controls · quests, factions and choices

The Arbiter tab governs which quests can enter Fate's assignment pool. Choose enabled content and quest categories, select a discovery mode, then use Save Director settings. A quest must pass the recorded restrictions to be drawn.

Set faction loyalties only after choosing a side in Skyrim; the Director then excludes opposing questlines. An unknown quest is never made eligible merely because it appears in the catalogue: you must discover it or explicitly approve it after checking in-game availability.

The Arbiter can also guide level-up attributes and eligible perk draws through Character. The Survivor's Choice / Fate's Choice / Shared Control modes belong specifically to the Spellbook, not to a single global control switch.

The Arbiter records and proposes decisions; it cannot operate Skyrim for you. You choose how many of these systems to use.

3. Use the Quest Journal · discover, draw, complete or block

All contains undiscovered catalogue quests; Discovered holds accepted quests (including active assignments); Completed includes finished and failed quests, including branching decisions and outcome notes. Manually suspended objectives are kept in the separate Blocked tab.

  1. When you encounter a quest in Skyrim, find it in All and choose Discover. Use search, category, faction and alphabetical filters to browse.
  2. Choose Draw Objective on the Dashboard or under Arbiter when ready. Fate chooses from the quests currently eligible under your saved rules.
  3. After resolving the objective in-game, record Complete. For a quest that actually fails in Skyrim, choose Mark failed in its expanded details. With Friends Like These... has its own Astrid outcome controls. If an objective is only temporarily unavailable, choose Suspend / Block.

Optional Strict Assignment Mode is under Arbiter → Expedition rules. It prevents recording deliberate completions of unassigned quests, while allowing confirmed automatic or unavoidable resolutions. World events can be recorded as soon as they happen.

For branching quests, expand the quest card and record the outcome you chose in Skyrim. The Arbiter excludes verified opposing paths but never chooses the moral decision. You can also save an outcome note for other quests.

The catalogue cannot guarantee a quest is currently startable. Some NPC deaths and faction choices can block later quest starts; use the Cross-quest consequences panel to record them and confirm exceptions in Skyrim. Approval is your confirmation, not an automatic unlock.

4. Track character progression · levels, skills and perks

Update the saved character level and attributes to match your game. Use the Level-Up Director if you want Fate to choose from permitted attributes; apply its choice yourself in Skyrim.

Record skill increases and purchased perks in Character → Skills & Perks. Fate uses those recorded skill levels, perk prerequisites and your Perk Focus to find eligible perk draws. Changing focus does not delete recorded purchases or learned spells.

Racial starting skills are applied automatically only to a new, unprogressed character. For an established character, changing race preserves recorded skills unless you deliberately apply the racial starting-skill reset.

5. Spellbook and Dragon Shouts · learned versus permitted

In Spellbook, mark spells learned in Skyrim. This is separate from whether a spell is currently permitted by your expedition's optional magic rules. With Follow Perk Focus enabled, the selected magic-school pool comes from your Character focus.

Survivor's Choice lets you choose permitted schools. Fate's Choice or Shared Control lets Fate choose; after selecting either mode, press Let Fate decide to generate the binding school selection. The mode and decision save separately. Quest-linked spell locks are optional.

In Shouts, track every Word of Power as Discovered when learned and Unlocked when available for use. Shouts are not governed by Perk Focus or Spellbook permissions. Some special shouts require all three words before they work.

6. Save, back up and restore · browser data and JSON

Recorded decisions save automatically in your browser's local storage. Export expedition backup (.json) in Settings whenever you reach a meaningful milestone; keep that file somewhere safe. The export includes your character, quests, magic, shouts and Chronicle.

To move to a new downloaded HTML, browser, device or GitHub Pages site, open the destination and use Import an expedition to restore your JSON. Import replaces the destination's current expedition after confirmation, so export it first if needed.

The Chronicle records expedition decisions and provides Undo last change for the current session. Undo does not replace a separate backup. Local downloads and hosted pages may not share browser storage or synchronise automatically.

7. If something looks wrong · quick checks

No eligible objectives? Check installed content in Settings, enabled quest categories and discovery mode under Arbiter, missing prerequisites, faction allegiance and manually blocked quests. The Arbiter's readiness test explains the current pool.

Fate's Choice shows no new magic decision? Select the mode, then press Let Fate decide. Your focus pool still applies until a binding draw has been made.

Active quest suddenly flagged? The dashboard now shows assignment-health warnings when recorded choices affect an active objective. Check Skyrim before resolving or suspending it.

Progress missing in a newer file? Check whether you opened a different browser or file location, then restore a saved JSON backup. Saved expeditions do not automatically transfer between origins.

Saving and Backups

Automatic saving: Your expedition is stored in this browser’s local storage as you make recorded decisions. It is not written into your Skyrim save file.

Keep an export: Browser storage can be cleared, blocked or isolated between browsers, devices, website addresses and local-file locations. A JSON backup is your portable copy.

How to move or restore an expedition
  1. In your current copy, select Export expedition backup and save the JSON file somewhere safe.
  2. Open the new Director HTML file, or the hosted Director page, in the browser where you want to continue.
  3. Under Import an expedition, select your saved JSON file and confirm replacing the expedition currently open there.
  4. Check your character, level, Quest Journal, Spellbook, Shouts and Chronicle. Export a fresh backup after significant progress.

A hosted page and a downloaded HTML file may use different browser-storage locations. Opening the same expedition in two locations does not automatically synchronise their changes. JSON backups are ordinary readable files; store them securely.

Import replaces the expedition in this browser after you confirm. The Director can import compatible earlier version-1 expedition backups; unsupported or malformed files are rejected. Export your current progress first.

Starting a new expedition replaces local progress and requires two confirmations. Undo is session-based and is not a substitute for a saved backup.

About The Fate Director

PUBLIC RELEASE · v2.2.1

Skyrim — The Fate Director is Survivor Incognito’s standalone expedition companion. The Arbiter of Fate is the name of the guiding presence behind Fate’s optional decisions, with its quest-decision controls under the Arbiter tab. You explore Skyrim; the Director helps you record progress and lets The Arbiter choose from eligible objectives and other decisions under rules you select.

Unofficial fan-made companion; not affiliated with or endorsed by Bethesda. No game files, mods or installation are required.

What Fate can control

Depending on your selections: eligible quest assignments, level-up attributes, available perk choices, and optional magic permissions. You can also track factions, discovered and completed quests, perks, learned spells, Dragon Shouts and expedition history.

What stays with the player

You choose when to accept decisions, how much authority Fate has, what you discover naturally and how faithfully to apply its suggestions. Starting playstyle equipment guidance is not an automatic restriction. The Director cannot read, edit or control your Skyrim save or game world.

Supported game content and limits

Edition choices cover Original, Legendary, Special and Anniversary Edition, with selectable DLC and Creation content. The catalogue is curated rather than an exhaustive authoritative database. Some quest start conditions, acquisitions and modded variants still need in-game confirmation; the Director deliberately requires manual approval for uncertain undiscovered quests.
